The problem

When small promises to yourself keep slipping, you stop trusting your own word. Habit apps answer with streaks and red marks, so one missed day turns into shame.

Hypothesis

If every kept promise becomes something you can see, a glowing orb in a jar, people will rebuild self-trust from evidence instead of pressure.

How it works

Calm by design: no streaks, no overdue, no red marks
  1. 01PromiseMake one to three small promises to yourself. Few on purpose, so it never becomes a to-do list.
  2. 02KeepEach kept promise drops a glowing orb into your jar. Twelve orbs fill it.
  3. 03Look backA full jar is sealed and kept on your shelf, in the style it was filled in.
Evidence, not pressureThe jar shows how many promises you kept, never how fast or how many you missed.
Letting go countsReleasing a promise is as valid as keeping it. How long one sat untouched stays hidden.
Jars and orbsSeveral jar styles, themes and orb shapes. Sealed jars keep the look they were filled in.
Optional AIWhen a promise is stuck, it asks what makes it hard, never “you have to”. Nothing is added unless you accept it.
