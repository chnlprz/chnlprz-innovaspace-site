AuraSync

Your local wellness hub.

In discovery · Early concept
Stage
Early concept
Platform
Planned: web and mobile
Role
Research, design, build
Focus
Local discovery, curated

The problem

Wellness options are scattered: coaches on social media, events on separate pages, spas on maps, retreats on travel sites. Finding the right fit takes too long.

Hypothesis

If coaches, events, places and products live in one place, matched to what you need, people will try more wellness options close to home.

How it works

One place, matched to you
  1. 01Tell usA short survey on what you need right now: rest, movement, someone to talk to, or something to learn.
  2. 02DiscoverSee coaches, events, wellness centers, spas and retreats near you.
  3. 03KeepSave coaches, follow recurring events and use exclusive offers.
Matched recommendationsSuggestions shaped by short surveys and what you save.
Nearby and savedPlaces and coaches close to you, plus a list of favorites.
Groups and retreatsRecurring classes, small groups and getaways.
Products and offersLinks to wellness products and apps, with member deals.
