Noggin?

Stay curious. A place for the thoughts you don’t want to lose.

The problem

Curious thoughts arrive when you can’t stop to look them up. They get forgotten, scattered across notes or lost in open tabs.

Hypothesis

If saving a thought takes seconds and old ones come back gently, people will return to their curiosity instead of losing it, and act on the ones that matter.

How it works

Calm by design: no points, streaks or red counts
  1. 01Catch itSave a Curiosity (an idea) or a Wondering (a question) in seconds. No account needed.
  2. 02Meet it againOnce a day at most, an old thought you haven’t opened comes back as a quiet Still curious? card.
  3. 03Act on itBreak it into next steps and send them to Nudge!, where they become tasks.
Curiosities and WonderingsIdeas and questions get their own homes, so neither gets lost in the other.
Suggested categoriesLearning, Ideas, Work or Life is picked for you. Change it with one tap.
Still curious?One old thought a day, never more. Open it, snooze it or archive it.
Steps to Nudge!Send next steps as tasks. Signed in, Noggin? shows when each one is done.
