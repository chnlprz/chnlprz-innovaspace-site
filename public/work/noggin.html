The problem

Curious thoughts arrive when you can’t stop to look them up. They get forgotten, scattered across notes or lost in open tabs.

Hypothesis

If saving a question takes seconds and the first answer is short and clear, people will come back to their curiosity instead of losing it.

How it works

Save first, explore later
  1. 01CaptureType or say a question in your own words. One tap to save.
  2. 02SortNoggin suggests a category. Change it anytime, or ignore it.
  3. 03ExploreWhen you have time, get a short, clear answer saved with the question.
Thought NuggetsSmall saved questions you can browse, revisit and pick up later.
Suggested categoriesHelps you find things again without planning folders.
Quick researchA concise first answer instead of a long list of links.
Sync to Nudge!When curiosity becomes a plan, send it to Nudge! as steps.
