The problem

Task apps expect you to organize while you capture. On tired days that’s too much, so the app gets abandoned and missed tasks start to feel like failure.

What Nudge! does

Capture first and sort later. The right task rises at the right time, and effort is rewarded instead of streaks, so a slow week never costs you progress.

Decisions that changed

What I changed after using v1 every day, and why
  1. RebuildStart over, on paper firstv1 grew as one HTML file. For 2.0 I wrote every rule and the data model down first, then rebuilt in reviewed phases.
  2. RewardsEffort, not completion1 point per 15 minutes of effort, and multi-day tasks are checked off per day, so big tasks still pay off along the way.
  3. RanksAlchemy to constellationsLevels come from lifetime XP and never drop. Stardust is spent separately, so spending never costs you a level.
  4. CaptureCategories became optionalAnything unpicked is Uncategorized. Sorting can wait, so capture stays instant on tired days.
  5. EnergyYour answer always winsThe optional cycle-based estimate only suggests. The daily check-in overrides it and feeds Analytics.
  6. AccountsOffline first, sign-up optionalEverything works on the device. Passkeys and sync are there for people who want more than one device.

Make it yours

Full when you want it. A plain list when you don’t.

Every layer is optional: the Task Banner, the widgets, points and rewards, the energy check-in. Switch them off and Nudge! becomes a calm, simple task list. No pressure. The point is that anyone can use it, whatever their habits need.

What I tested and learned

From my own daily use of 2.6, written down as I went
Reminders didn’t always arrive.I missed a 9 PM briefing and a timed test task.2.7: reminders go out within a minute, late-added tasks are scheduled, and Settings shows what’s coming up and what was sent.
I couldn’t find the briefing time.It lived only under Home screen settings.2.7: it’s also in Notifications, where I looked first.
The briefing notice stayed after I tapped it.It should clear and take me to tomorrow.2.7: it dismisses and opens Upcoming, from the push too.
Lists didn’t behave the same.Upcoming items weren’t tappable, and points showed in some lists only.2.7: tap any Upcoming item for details; points show in every list and the calendar, and hide when points are off.
Long names got cut off in the banner.The most important task was the one I couldn’t read.2.7: long names scroll gently.
Still open.A confirmation before redeeming a reward, a full points history, sorting in the Matrix and Task Demand, where Anytime tasks belong, and a fair bonus for finishing dated tasks early.In progress for the next release.

Why it’s different

Built on behavior science, not willpower. It plans for low-energy days instead of punishing them.
