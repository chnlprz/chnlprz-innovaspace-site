Nudge!

A priority tracker built for the tired version of you.

The problem

Most task apps assume you have energy to organize while you capture. At the end of a long day you don't, so the app gets abandoned. I wanted a system that fits my real habits instead of one I'd quit in a week.

The approach

Capture first, sort later. Tasks land in Unsorted with an optional batch date, then get sorted into an Eisenhower Matrix when there's headspace. A nightly check-in in Upcoming briefs you for tomorrow before sleep. Rewards are based on effort (1 point per 15 minutes), and constellation levels only go up, so a slow week never costs you progress.

What's next

Passkey sign-in and push notifications, then an AI module: speak a batch of tasks, let it suggest priorities, and confirm them in one tap. The long-term goal is a native mobile app.

How I design

Psychology, applied

Design for the tired version of you

Capture should take seconds. Organizing can wait for a day when you have the energy.

Reward showing up

No streaks to break and no progress to lose. Effort counts, even on slow days.

You stay in charge

Suggestions are always editable, and nothing turns into a task unless you decide it should.

Patterns, not diagnoses

Analytics describe what you logged. They help you notice things without telling you what's wrong with you.
